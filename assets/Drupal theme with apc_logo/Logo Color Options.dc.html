Color options from the logo

Three ways to build a Drupal accent palette from the armadillo mark — pick one, or ask for a mix.

Option A
Shell & Sky

The armadillo's brown shell as the primary accent, its teal background as a cool complementary second accent — replaces the system's default sage with a hue lifted straight from the logo.

Option B
Bandana

The collar's brick red leads, with the same teal background as its cool counterpoint. Bold and high-contrast — best if the site wants a strong, memorable accent.

Option C
Desert Warm

Closest to Organic's own terracotta default, but tuned to the logo's actual shell tone, with the collar red as an occasional pop accent rather than a second full ramp.

All three keep Organic's cream ground, Caprasimo/Figtree type, radii and shadows — only the accent hues change, drawn from the logo's shell brown, collar red and teal background.
